Privacy

This page explains what happens to your data when you open a link on this website.

What is recorded

When you open a link, this website records:

  • your IP address,
  • the date and time,
  • which link you opened and where you were sent,
  • whether the visit was treated as automated or low-quality traffic, and why.

To recognize automated traffic, the website also checks your browser's identification (User-Agent) and language headers, and looks up the country and network of your IP address. These are not stored. The lookup uses a database on this website's own server; your IP address is not sent to anyone for it.

Why, and on what basis

The data is used to count visits, to keep bots and fraudulent traffic away from advertisers, and to keep the service secure. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in preventing fraud and protecting the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Cookie

Before forwarding you, the website may show a short "Redirecting…" page that sets one cookie ({{ cookie_name }}). It only confirms that your browser passed an automated-traffic check, contains no personal data, is not used for tracking, and expires after {{ cookie_days }} days.
